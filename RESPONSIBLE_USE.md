# Responsible Use (Required Reading)

Reconnect is intended to help people **lawfully** search for and reconnect with loved ones.

## ✅ Allowed / Intended Uses
- Reconnecting with family or friends you’ve lost contact with.
- Genealogy research and historical family searches.
- Organizing documentation for **law enforcement, attorneys, courts, or licensed investigators**.
- Welfare checks and missing-person reporting **through proper authorities**.

## ❌ Prohibited Uses
Do **not** use Reconnect to:
- Stalk, harass, intimidate, or surveil anyone.
- Dox people (publish or share private info without consent).
- Locate protected persons (e.g., DV survivors, minors, witnesses) outside official channels.
- Bypass logins, paywalls, anti-bot measures, or any website’s Terms of Service.
- Automate scraping of databases or social media.

## Safety-by-Default
Reconnect is designed to be **local-first** and to **link out** to sources instead of scraping.
If you modify it, keep these safeguards:
- Default bind to `localhost` (not LAN)
- Redaction for phone/email/IDs
- Audit logging

## Important Note
Reconnect does **not** replace law enforcement.
If you believe someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
